FAQ

Fuel Monitoring Systems — Questions

How does a fuel monitoring system work?

A fuel level sensor is installed in the tank and connected to a GPS tracker. The tracker sends the fuel level and the vehicle's location over 4G to a web platform and phone app, where you can see the live level, history and alerts.

Can a fuel monitoring system detect fuel theft?

Yes. The system records the fuel level continuously, so a sudden drop — for example from siphoning — shows up clearly and can trigger an alert. Refills are also recorded, so you can compare them with fuel receipts.

Which vehicles and machines can be fitted?

Trucks, buses, construction machinery, agricultural vehicles and generators. The sensors work with both metal and plastic fuel tanks.

What is the difference between a capacitive and a BLE fuel sensor?

A capacitive sensor is a stainless steel probe inside the tank, wired to the GPS tracker. A BLE sensor sends its readings wirelessly over Bluetooth, which means fewer cables and a quicker installation. We will recommend the right one for your tanks.

Do I need a GPS tracker as well?

Yes — the fuel sensor connects to a GPS tracker that sends the data to the app. We supply and install both together as one system.
