FAQ

Security Systems — Questions

Do you install GPS trackers on motorcycles (boda bodas)?

Yes. We install GPS trackers on cars, motorcycles and trucks. The tracker is hidden and wired professionally, and you can see your vehicle's live location on your phone.

Can I watch my CCTV cameras on my phone?

Yes. When your CCTV system is connected to the internet we set up remote viewing on your smartphone, so you can watch your cameras from anywhere.

Which CCTV cameras do you install?

We supply and install genuine Hikvision cameras, including ColorVu cameras that show full-colour images at night and outdoor PTZ (pan-tilt-zoom) cameras for large areas. We install both IP and analog systems.

How much does electric fence installation cost?

The price depends on the length of your wall and the number of lines. Send us your location and wall size on WhatsApp and we will arrange a site visit and give you a quotation.

Do you work outside Kampala?

We are based in Kampala and install across Uganda. Tell us your location on WhatsApp or by phone and we will arrange the installation.
