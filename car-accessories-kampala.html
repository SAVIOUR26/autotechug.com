FAQ

Car Accessories & Systems — Questions

Why should I install a car alarm?

A car alarm scares thieves away with a loud siren, lets you lock the car with one click, protects the doors, boot and bonnet, locks the doors automatically while driving and helps you find your car in parking. Small money that protects a car worth millions.

Which cars can you fit Android screens to?

We fit Android screens to many models — including Toyota Prado, Harrier, Allion and Hiace, Subaru Forester, Nissan X-Trail and Mercedes-Benz — using the correct dash frame. Send us your car model and year to confirm.

What is a car secret button?

A secret (security) button is a hidden switch that must be pressed before the car will start. Even with the key, a thief who does not know where the button is cannot start the car.

Do your car alarms come with a guarantee?

Yes. Our car alarms are genuine, long-lasting units with a guarantee, installed with clean wiring and backed by 24/7 after-sale support.
