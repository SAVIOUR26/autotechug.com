FAQ

Solar Systems — Questions

Do solar security cameras need electricity or internet?

No mains electricity is needed — the camera runs on its solar panel and battery. To view it on your phone it needs a connection, using a 4G SIM card or Wi-Fi depending on the model.

How much are solar flood lights?

Our Blue Carbon solar flood lights come in three sizes: 1.0 at UGX 340,000, 2.0 at UGX 390,000 and 3.0 at UGX 400,000. Installation is available — contact us for a quote.

Do you install solar street lights for compounds and roads?

Yes. We supply and install both all-in-one solar street lights and high-power street lights with a separate solar panel, mounted on poles for compounds, gates, estates and roads.

Can you design a solar system for my home or business?

Yes. Tell us which appliances you want to power and we will recommend the right panels, inverter and batteries, then install the complete system.
